About

The Lower Palisades Restoration Project aims to restore and protect the forested Palisades cliffs across Hudson County. Although these cliffs form one of the areas largest remaining natural areas, they currently suffer from invasive plants, illegal dumping, and a lack of coordinated stewardship despite their importance as tree canopy and wildlife habitat. The project will begin with a Jersey City-owned parcel below Riverview Park as a pilot for a broader restoration effort across Hudson County. The goals of the project are cleanups on steep landscapes, invasive species remediation, native vegetation plantings, and community engagement.

A key challenge is building public awareness and long-term community support. In order to achieve this, we are partnering with existing organizations that promote nature such as birdwatching groups. The goal being to get more people to appreciate and understand the value of having more nature around them. The benefits are improved air quality, reduced urban heat island effects, and mental health benefits.
